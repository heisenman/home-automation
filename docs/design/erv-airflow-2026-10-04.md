## Reading it (with the 2026-10-04 afternoon baseline, dehumidifier blower OFF at 3 W)

Phase A was NOT "dehumidifier off": its VENT/air-cycling blower ran (86–88 W) throughout. The blower-off
comparison comes from the same afternoon's mode test (design §7.2 log): Med = supply 2082 RPM / 61 W.

1. **The dehumidifier does not limit fresh-air delivery.** Low and Med hit their CFM targets in every case. At
   High and Turbo the ceiling is the ERV's own EXHAUST fan, pegged at 3000 RPM, with supply RPM still ~2200.
2. **Turbo = High on this install** (~124 CFM both, exhaust-limited). High is the right dry-out floor; Turbo
   costs the same power for no extra air.
3. **The dehumidifier's blower ASSISTS the ERV supply:** Med supply RPM 2082 → 1149 and ERV power 61 → 43 W with
   the dehum blower running. Net cost of running it: +86 W − 19 W ≈ +67 W. Vent mode is an airflow/comfort
   choice, not an efficiency gain.
4. **Compressor on vs off makes no measurable difference** to the ERV (A vs B within 1–2 %): only the blower
   matters, so coil wetness isn't adding resistance worth designing around.
