## Decisions

1. **Indoor moisture = equal-weight mean of absolute humidity** over `meter_pro_living_room`,
   `meter_kitchen`, `meter_c_bed`, `meter_pro_c_office` and `meter_pro_h_bed` (720/720 hours complete in the
   last 30 days).
   - Left out: `meter_living_room` (a cold spot: 2.5 °C below the Pro, −0.7 g/m³), the H bed closet, both
     bathrooms (shower pulses, SD 0.9 g/m³ in the H bath, used as event flags instead), the attic, and the
     crawlspace. The attic and crawlspace are their own terms in the balance.
   - Room-to-room AH offsets of 0.2–0.5 g/m³ are steady (SD 0.1–0.3), so they're largely sensor calibration,
     not real differences. The study works on **changes** in AH, where fixed offsets cancel. Don't read
     sub-0.5 g/m³ room differences as real.
2. **Outdoor: the local meter is primary** from 2026-10-04 (Hugh: local beats the grid average).
   - Open-Meteo only backfills before Oct 4.
   - Local minus grid: T +0.05 °C on average but **+2.3–2.8 °C at midday and −1.2…−1.7 °C at night**; AH
     +0.41 g/m³ (RMS 1.15).
   - Backfill gets an hour-of-day bias correction. Treat its AH as ±1 g/m³, which is as large as typical
     indoor–outdoor differences. So the summer whole-house balance (method A) is weak, and **summer Midea
     efficiency rests on the bucket method (C)**.
   - Open question for Hugh: does the outdoor meter get midday sun? (A sun-warmed meter keeps AH roughly
     right but inflates T.)
3. **Aprilaire on/off comes from its power meter**, not `unit_running`.
   - `unit_running` and `unit_rh_pct` have a 286-hour hole (Oct 6 10:00 → ~Oct 18 PT, the RS-485/REMOTE
     changeover). `dh_call` and `dehum_pm` are complete.
   - Running hours average 409 W, off hours 3.5 W.
   - 4 "running" hours averaged < 150 W (Oct 20, 22, 23, all 03:00–07:00): fan without compressor. Check them
     against the E070's 50 °F / 40 °F-dew-point floor in Phase 1.
4. **Midea:**
   - Mode is reliable (99.6 % of hours; one 8 h gap on Jul 26). Mode 2 = 461 W, mode 1 = 3.0 W, matching the
     back-calculation.
   - Onboard RH is unusable (−11.4 % vs the control sensor).
   - Onboard T is usable with **+0.84 °C** (SD 0.48).
5. **BME680 T/RH are unusable** (self-heated: +9…+11 °C, −16…−19 % RH). Their AH is roughly right (±0.45) but
   not needed.
6. **Crawlspace:** the meter and the Aranet agree (ΔT −0.02 °C, ΔAH +0.27 g/m³). Either works; use the meter.
7. **Study meters (Midea inlet/outlet):** only 2 settled hours so far. Collect a day of Midea-idle hours to fix
   the pair's zero offset before trusting run deltas (first look: T within 0.1 °C, RH within 1 %).
8. **Coverage is otherwise excellent:** every room sensor 100 % of hours since Jun 24 (one 1 h gap on Jul 22);
   ERV, blower and thermostat complete since they started.
